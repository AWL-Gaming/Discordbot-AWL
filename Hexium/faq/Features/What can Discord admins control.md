Configured Discord admins can use commands for player and server administration, including player lists and stats, inventory and kill information, kick and ban controls, item grants, teleporting, spawning prefabs, environment changes, events, status effects, healing, skills, world saves, broadcasts, sleep, global keys, whispers, music, and installed-mod checks.

Availability and permissions are shown by `!help`. Admin-only commands require the sender to be listed in `Discord Admin` under `[4 - Commands]`.
